# Intake — what to ask before saying anything

Route first with A1–A3 and D14 (who, nationality history, Indian passport status, state). Give the workflow, fee and first to-dos from those. Ask the rest only as each answer would change the plan; never dump all twenty at once. Unknown answers go to "Refuse unknown slots" (SKILL.md).

## A. Who is applying
1. Who is this for: you, your spouse, a child, a parent? One application per person; list every person.
2. For each person: current nationality, and how it was acquired (naturalization date; born abroad; by parents).
3. Did this person ever hold an Indian passport? If yes: is it still in hand, and was it ever cancelled or surrendered (stamp, certificate, CKGS-era)?
4. Any OCI already held by anyone in the household (number, name on it, passport it was issued against)?

## B. Names and dates (where applications die)
5. Exact name on the last Indian passport. Exact name on the current US passport. Exact name on the naturalization certificate. Exact name on the birth certificate (child) and the mother's name as printed there.
6. Any difference at all, including a middle name, an initial, order, spelling, a dropped "-bhai"/"-ben"? Each difference needs a paper: marriage certificate, court order, or a notarized one-and-the-same-person affidavit.
7. Date of birth on the Indian passport vs the US passport. A mismatch is a rejection with no fix; stop and tell the user.
8. Place of birth as written on both.

## C. Papers in hand
9. US passport expiry (need ≥ 9 months at submission to satisfy every checklist).
10. Marriage certificate: civil registrar or religious? Which state/country issued it? Apostilled?
11. Child's birth certificate: long form naming both parents, or hospital/short form? Which state? Apostilled?
12. Naturalization certificate in hand (not lost)?
13. Proof of address: which document, dated when, does it match the address you will put on the form, and does it match where the courier returns to?

## D. Where and how
14. State of residence → consulate jurisdiction (`centres.md`). Which VFS centre is nearest: walk-in or mail?
15. Travel to India planned in the next 4 months? (Surrender → OCI is sequential; the gap needs a visa. See `recovery.md`.)
16. Both parents available to sign in front of a notary (minor)? If not: sole-custody order, death certificate, or single-parent adoption?

## E. Photo and files
17. Does a digital photo exist, or only a pharmacy print? Background colour? Date taken?
18. Can the user scan to PDF at ≤ 500 KB per document? Phone scanner apps count.

## F. Calendar and tracking
19. Which calendar (Google / Apple / Outlook) and may the agent write to it?
20. Which email receives VFS and consulate mail; may the agent watch it for "deficiency", "on hold", "returned", "Granted"?

## Decision tree after intake
- Indian passport held, not cancelled → **surrender first** (`surrender.md`). Nothing else can be filed.
- Cancelled before 1 Jun 2010 with stamp → no surrender certificate needed; the stamped passport is the proof.
- Naturalized ≥ 10 years ago and passport lost → Declaration of Renunciation route (`surrender.md`).
- Surrender certificate in hand → fresh OCI (`oci-fresh.md`).
- US-born child, parent has cancelled Indian passport or OCI → `oci-minor.md`.
- Foreign-origin spouse → `oci-spouse.md` (2-year registered marriage bar) else `visa-spouse.md`.
- OCI held, new passport → `oci-maintenance.md` (upload vs re-issue).
- Any name or DOB mismatch → fix the paper before any form.
