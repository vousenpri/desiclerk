            Gotchas

            Things nobody tells you.

            Don't print the form from Chrome. The photo loads late and Chrome prints it blank. Use Safari or Firefox, wait until you see the photo, then print.

            You may not need the notarized "signature change" letter. It isn't on the official list. New York has asked for it; New Jersey did not in October 2026. Ask your centre before paying a notary.

            The passport surrender costs $62. Older guides say it depends on when you became a citizen. It doesn't any more.

            VFS gives two different photo sizes. A 600 × 600 JPEG under 30 KB works for both. The photo check above makes one.

            Don't fill the OCI form early. You can't send it until the certificate is back, and it expires 90 days after you fill it.

            There is no OCI card any more. Since July 2026 you download a PDF when the website says Granted. Old cards still work. Consulate General of India, Chicago, notice of 9 July 2026.

            Your child's birth certificate needs an apostille. Apostilled means notarised by a government. The hospital one won't do, and the short one won't do. In New York it's three offices, all close together: get the long form at 45 Lafayette Street, Room 133 (book first, and say it's for an apostille); get the signature checked at 60 Centre Street; get the apostille at 123 William Street. Then send a signed copy, not the original. Same for your marriage certificate if it's from the US. Check the mother's name is spelled the same on the birth certificate and her passport. That mismatch is the most common reason a child's OCI gets sent back.

            Any change to your name counts. A dropped middle name, one letter, a new surname. Put the old name in the OCI form as your previous name and bring a notarized letter saying both names are you. Miss this and they send it back.
