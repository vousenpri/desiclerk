Verified 2026-10-27 against the VFS USA one-pager and the "New OCI – Adult – Foreign National by Naturalization" checklist.

## Preconditions (before opening ociservices.gov.in)
- Renunciation/surrender certificate in hand (if passport surrendered after 31 May 2010). **Cannot apply for OCI and surrender together.**
- US passport valid ≥ 6 months at submission.
- Photo and signature JPEGs already made to spec (see `photo-specs.md`). The portal asks for them mid-form.
- All supporting documents already scanned to PDF ≤ 500 KB, one file per category.
- Date of birth on US passport must match last Indian passport exactly. Mismatch = rejection, no fix.
- Any name difference of **any kind** (surname at marriage, middle name → initial, one letter, order of names, "-bhai" dropped) must be entered as "previous name" on the form and backed by a notarized name-change affidavit or court order. Omitting it is a listed rejection cause.
- Form expires 90 days after completion; VFS recommends a fresh form if older than 70 days at submission.
- Once submitted online, nothing can be edited. Errors → new form.

## Checklist — adult (intake order)
1. OCI application form printed from the portal, Part B signed. Original.
2. 2 photos affixed (2x2, light non-white bg) + digital photo and signature uploaded; physical and digital identical.
3. Current US passport — self-attested copy of info page + amendment pages (do **not** send the original).
4. Last Indian passport — copy of first 2 and last 2 pages. If unavailable, the documents used for the renunciation.
5. Renunciation / surrender certificate — copy (or cancelled passport with "cancelled on acquiring foreign citizenship" stamp if surrendered on or before 31 May 2010).
6. Naturalization certificate — copy, signed at the designated space. Via parents → their certificates + notarized affidavit by both parents.
7. Proof of marital status — marriage certificate (civil registrar; **apostilled if issued outside India**, so a US marriage certificate needs the state apostille; self-attested copy, never the original) / divorce decree / spouse's death certificate; notarized affidavit if unavailable. See `apostille.md`.
8. Name change document — notarized one-and-the-same-person affidavit or court order. Condition-based but very commonly triggered. A chain of changes (maiden name on the Indian passport → married name → middle name re-added at naturalization) is covered by the marriage certificate + naturalization certificate + **one** affidavit listing every version; enter the Indian-passport name as "previous name" on the form.
9. Employment / work letter (or self-employment proof, retirement proof, admission letter, or a written statement if never employed).
10. OCI cards of parents and spouse — copies if any.
11. Previous Indian visa — copy if any.
12. Proof of address — one of state ID, driver's licence (uncropped), utility bill ≤ 3 months, lease, mortgage, tax return, dorm letter. PO Box refused.
13. Affidavit in lieu of originals — notarized (VFS format).
14. Undertaking by the OCI applicant (VFS format).
14a. **Undertaking for surrender of Indian voter card and ration card** (separate VFS form). Not on the fresh-adult checklist, but applications have been held for it (immihelp, 2025-06). Sign it even if the user never held either.
15. Consent letter authorising the consulate to correct errors (VFS format). Not a guarantee of correction.
16. VFS application confirmation / payment receipt. Courier: UPS or USPS label with no expiry; FedEx refused. Return address must match address proof.
17. Checklist printed and signed as cover page.

Non-US passport holders add: notarized colour copy of green card / EAD / I-797A / visa, and native-country address proof.

## Listed rejection causes (verbatim categories from the checklist)
1. Poor-quality uploaded photo or signature.
2. Not all supporting documents uploaded online.
3. Wrong passport number or date of issue.
4. Wrong name / place of birth / date of birth.
5. Parents' or spouse's full legal names missing or not matching.
6. Marital status / spouse name not entered.
7. Wrong phone or email.
8. Part B unsigned (or, for minors, unsigned by a parent).
9. Previous name not entered where a name changed.
10. Incomplete postal address (ZIP missing).
11. Native address proof missing (non-US nationals).

## Re-upload
If photo/signature/documents fail, they can be re-uploaded on the portal using the file reference number printed vertically on the left and below the barcode of page 1 of the submitted form.

## Timing
Typically 8–10 weeks; verification stages in India. **No physical card since 8 July 2026**: the grant is an e-OCI PDF downloaded from ociservices.gov.in once status reads Granted. Existing booklets stay valid. Track at ociservices.gov.in → Status Enquiry (application number + passport number) and on the VFS tracker.

## Minor variants
- Minor (born in US, never held Indian passport): parents' surrendered Indian passports (first and last pages with cancellation stamp) or OCI cards; birth certificate naming both parents; parents' marriage certificate; notarized parental authorisation; parents' US status if non-US. Thumb impression if the child cannot sign; parents must **not** sign in the applicant's box.
