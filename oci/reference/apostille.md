# Apostille — what it is, which papers need it, how to get one (USA)

Verified 2026-10-28 against the VFS "Minor – New – Foreign national by birth" checklist (Sep 2026) and nyc.gov / NY Dept of State pages. Plain words for the user: **an apostille is a notarisation by a government.** The state confirms the signature on a public record is real, so a foreign government (India) will accept the paper.

## Which papers need it for OCI
| Paper | Apostille? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| US-issued birth certificate (child's OCI) | **Yes** | VFS, verbatim: born outside India → "must either be apostilled by the relevant foreign government or attested by the appropriate foreign mission." Long form naming both parents; hospital certificates refused. |
| US-issued marriage certificate (adult OCI, minor OCI, spouse OCI) | **Yes** | VFS: marriage certificate "issued outside India… must be apostilled or attested." Civil registrar certificate only; temple/church certificates refused. Needed on an adult's own OCI as proof of marital status and to explain a name change at marriage. |
| Naturalization certificate | No | Self-attested copy only. Do not apostille it (USCIS forbids copying it for most purposes, but VFS asks for the copy; sign where the checklist says). |
| Indian passport, surrender certificate, US passport | No | Copies. |
| Name-change affidavit (one-and-the-same-person) | No, notarized | Ordinary US notary. One affidavit covers a chain of changes (e.g. maiden → married → naturalization re-adds middle name). |
| Parental authorization | No, notarized in person | Wet-ink notary. E-notary refused (Atlanta 2025-01, Chicago 2024-09 and 2026-01). |
| Entry (X) visa documents | No | The X-visa checklist does not ask for apostilles. |

**Never send the apostilled original.** VFS: "Do not send original apostilled documents… We require self-attested copies of the apostilled documents instead." Copy the whole thing (certificate + apostille page), sign the copy.

## How to get one — New York City birth certificate (the chain)
1. **Long-form birth certificate with letter of exemplification.** NYC Health, 45 Lafayette St, Room 133, by appointment; or VitalChek online / the mail form, request reason **"Apostille/Authentication"** (the letter is issued only for that reason). $15 + vendor fee. The short certificate will not be apostilled.
2. **County Clerk authentication** of the registrar's signature. New York County Clerk, 60 Centre St, Room 141B. $3. Walk-in.
3. **Apostille.** NY Department of State, 123 William St (2nd floor), or by mail. $10 per document.
Same-day is possible in person if you start early; all three are within a few blocks.

## Other states
Birth certificate from the state or county vital-records office (long form, both parents named) → the state Secretary of State's apostille office. Some states skip the county-clerk step. Marriage certificate: from the issuing county clerk → state apostille. Check the Secretary of State page for the state that issued the paper; the apostille must come from the issuing state, not the state you live in.

## Agent rules
- Treat the apostille as a **precondition** with its own clock (ordering + mail can be 2–6 weeks). Put it at the top of the OCI to-do, before the form is filled.
- Ask the user which state issued each certificate; route to that state's apostille office.
- Do not tell the user to apostille a naturalization certificate or passports.
- Marriage certificate goes on the adult's own OCI stack **and** the child's. One apostilled original, copied twice.
