# Fresh OCI — spouse of foreign origin of an Indian citizen or OCI holder (USA, via VFS)

Verified 2026-10-27 against:
- VFS USA one-pager, OCI services (eligibility wording, fees, courier): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/
- VFS checklist "New – Foreign spouse applicant" (Sep 2026): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/pdf/New-Foreign-spouse-applicant-sep-2026.pdf (older undated version: `Foreign-spouse-applicant.pdf`)
- VFS "Sworn Affidavit" (joint marriage affidavit format): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/pdf/sworn-affidavit.pdf
- CGI Chicago "How to apply for OCI Card" (interview rule): https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/content/How-to-apply-for-OCI-Card-OCI-2026.docx.pdf
- Embassy of India, Washington — reissue guidelines (spouse upload rule): https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/pages/NjI
- Statute: Citizenship Act 1955, s.7A(1)(d) (eligibility) and s.7D(f) (cancellation on dissolution), as applied in LeeAnne Singh v. Union of India, Bombay HC, Oct 2020 (livelaw.in report).

## Eligibility (one-pager wording)
- Spouse of foreign origin of an Indian citizen, **or** of an OCI cardholder registered under s.7A.
- Marriage **registered** and **subsisting for a continuous period of at least two years immediately before** submission. Under two years → not eligible; use an Entry visa (`visa-spouse.md`).
- Subject to prior security clearance (statute; the consulate decides, the applicant does nothing).
- Not eligible if the applicant is / was a citizen of Pakistan or Bangladesh (statute).

## In-person interview
- **CGI Chicago:** applications under the Foreign Spouse category are accepted only after a **personal interview of the applicant and the spouse**. Consulate-level, not on the VFS checklist.
- Other US consulates publish no rule; NY has historically called couples in (interviewed separately, scheduled by email that can land in spam — immihelp report, lead only). Treat the interview as **likely at every US consulate; confirmed for Chicago**. Both spouses should keep availability open for the 6–8 weeks after submission and watch spam for the scheduling email.
- Outside the US (Paris, Auckland) both spouses must appear at submission; do not import that to a US VFS centre, which is drop-off/mail only.

## Preconditions
- Marriage certificate must be **registered**: married in India → Marriage Registrar certificate; married in the US → state-issued certificate, **apostilled** by that state's Secretary of State (US-origin documents still need the apostille on this checklist); married elsewhere → apostilled or attested by the Indian mission there. Non-English → certified translation. Send a self-attested copy of the apostilled document, not the original.
- **The Indian/OCI spouse's passport or OCI card must show the applicant's name as spouse.** If it does not, the spouse re-issues their document first. This is the hold that costs months; check before anything else.
- If the Indian spouse has become a foreign citizen: their renunciation/surrender certificate must exist (upload category), and they sign the voter-ID / ration-card surrender undertaking.
- Applicant's passport valid **≥ 9 months** at submission (Sep 2026 checklist; the older spouse checklist said 6).
- Photo not older than **9 months** per the Sep 2026 checklist (other OCI checklists say 6; use ≤ 6 and satisfy both). Print and upload identical. Specs in `photo-specs.md`.
- Applicant's own birth certificate, apostilled if foreign-issued; the checklist also asks for birth certificates of the applicant's parents/grandparents/great-grandparents where available (lineage check for the Pakistan/Bangladesh bar). UNVERIFIED how strictly US consulates enforce the ancestors' certificates; have the applicant's own at minimum.
- Both spouses available together before a notary for the joint affidavit.
- PDFs ≤ 1000 KB per category; everything uploaded.

## Listed rejection causes
Same eleven as the adult list. The spouse-specific ones: **#5 spouse's full legal name missing or not matching** (married name vs passport name — enter previous name), **#6 marital status / spouse name not entered**, plus the un-listed but decisive hold: applicant's name absent from the spouse's passport/OCI card.

## After grant — the ongoing obligations (embassy page)
- **Every new passport**, for life, regardless of age: upload on ociservices.gov.in → OCI Misc Services (Passport Particulars Updation) the new passport bio page, a recent photo, **a declaration that the marriage subsists**, and a copy of the Indian spouse's passport (or the OCI spouse's passport **and** card). Free. Within 3 months of receiving the passport. The age-20/age-50 rules do not apply to the spouse category.
- Name change after marriage → paid re-issue (`oci-reissue.md`), with the joint affidavit again (item 9 of that checklist).
- Paris and Auckland consulate pages say a spouse OCI is valid 5 years and must be renewed with a fresh application and interview. **UNVERIFIED for the USA** — no US consulate or VFS page states a validity period. Do not quote it; note it as drift.

## Loss of OCI
- s.7D(f), Citizenship Act 1955: the Government may cancel an OCI granted under s.7A(1)(d) if the marriage **has been dissolved by a competent court or otherwise**, or if during its subsistence the cardholder **marries another person**. Bombay HC (Oct 2020) upheld cancellation even with a divorce appeal pending.
- The VFS joint affidavit extends this by undertaking to **return the card on divorce or on the death of either spouse**. The statute does not list death; the affidavit does. Treat the card as surrenderable on either event.
- An OCI holder who later becomes an Indian citizen's ex-spouse but is independently of Indian origin can re-apply in the ordinary category.
