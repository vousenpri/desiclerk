# Fresh OCI — adult born outside India, never held an Indian passport (OCI by descent)

Verified 2026-10-30 against the VFS USA checklist "Adult – foreign national by birth and never held Indian passport" (Sep 2026): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/pdf/Adult-foreign-national-by-birth-and-never-held-Indian-passport-adult-sep-2026.pdf and the OCI one-pager.

Who this is: the US-born adult child (or grandchild, or great-grandchild) of someone who was an Indian citizen. Typically 18–40, parents naturalized long ago, has been "meaning to do it" for years. **Nothing to surrender.** The whole application turns on one thing: proving the parent's (or grandparent's) Indian origin.

## Preconditions (before opening ociservices.gov.in)
- **Proof of Indian origin in hand.** Any ONE of, as self-attested copies: the parent's/grandparent's Indian passport (first 2 and last 2 pages; a cancelled/surrendered one is fine, the cancellation stamp helps); a domicile certificate; a nativity certificate; the parent's OCI plus the documents it was issued on. If the direct proof is gone, the documents must build the chain (e.g. grandparent's passport + parent's birth certificate naming the grandparent). "Failure to establish lineage" is a listed rejection cause. This is the slow item: if the passport is in a cupboard in India, start there.
- **Own birth certificate**, long form, naming both parents with their DOB and nationality, **apostilled** by the issuing state. Hospital certificate refused. Self-attested copy of the apostilled original; never the original.
- **Parents' marriage certificate**, civil registrar, apostilled if issued outside India (US-issued → state apostille). Religious certificates refused. Required when the claim runs through the parents.
- US passport valid ≥ 9 months at submission.
- Own marital status proof if married/divorced/widowed (marriage certificate apostilled if foreign-issued, etc.).
- Any name difference across the chain (applicant's name vs birth certificate; parent's name on the passport vs on the applicant's birth certificate; mother's maiden vs married name) → notarized one-and-the-same-person affidavit, and "previous name" entered on the form. Rejection cause #5 (parents' names not matching) is the usual failure in this category.
- Photo (pale, not white) and signature JPEGs to spec; all documents scanned to PDF ≤ 1000 KB (target 500), one per portal category. Missing uploads = returned.
- Form expires 90 days after completion; nothing editable after submission.

## Portal upload categories that matter here
"Indian origin proof", "Current/previous Indian passport of parents/grandparents", "Nativity certificate", "Domicile certificate", "OCI card of parents", "Marriage certificate" (parents' goes here too), "Relationship certificate". Anything with no category → upload under "current passport".

## Listed rejection causes
Same eleven as the adult checklist (`oci-fresh.md`). The ones that bite in this category: #4 (name/POB/DOB not exactly as on the current passport), #5 (parents' full names not matching their current legal names), #9 (previous name not mentioned), and lineage not established.

## Timing
8–10 weeks after VFS receipt; e-OCI PDF at "Granted". No surrender clock, so the apostille (2–6 weeks) and getting the parent's passport copy are the long poles. Plan: papers first, form last.

## Agent rules for this category
- Ask first: whose Indian passport can you get a copy of, and where is it? That answer decides everything.
- Build the name chain explicitly: applicant on birth certificate → applicant on passport; parent on Indian passport → parent on applicant's birth certificate → parent on current passport/OCI. Every mismatch gets a paper.
- Both apostilles (own birth certificate, parents' marriage certificate) go on the to-do before the form. Route by issuing state (`apostille.md`).
- No surrender step. Do not put surrender items on this user's list.
