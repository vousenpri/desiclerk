# Entry visa (X) for a non-Indian spouse or child of a person of Indian origin (USA, via VFS)

Verified 2026-10-27 against:
- VFS USA one-pager, Visa services — Entry Visa (Miscellaneous Visa-X): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/visa-services/
- VFS "Entry Visa Checklist" (Sep 2025, updated): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/visa-services/pdf/Entry-Visa-Revised-Sep-2025-updated.pdf
- Embassy of India, Washington — "Revised Visa fees for USA nationals": https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/extra?id=90 (undated)
- Embassy "Apply Indian Visa" page (ICWF, VFS charge wording): https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/extra?id=124

A **regular paper visa**, filed on indianvisaonline.gov.in and submitted to VFS. Not an e-visa and not a tourist visa: the tourist visa does not cover living with a spouse or long stays, and the X visa is what immigration expects a foreign spouse to hold.

## When it beats OCI
| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Married **< 2 years** (OCI spouse bar) | Entry visa |
| Need to travel inside ~2 months (OCI is 6–10 weeks + possible interview) | Entry visa now, OCI later |
| Child of an OCI/PIO parent whose OCI paperwork (apostilles, parental authorization) is not ready | Entry visa |
| Applicant or ancestor is/was Pakistani or Bangladeshi (OCI barred) | Entry visa, consulate decides duration |
| OCI card surrendered for re-issue and travel unavoidable | Entry visa |
| Married ≥ 2 years, no urgency | OCI (`oci-spouse.md`): lifelong, no re-application |

Processing: **15 calendar days** after the consulate receives it; one-pager warns 30–60 days in exceptional cases. Validity: the one-pager advertises "5 years, not just 6 months / 1 year" for this category; the consulate fixes the actual duration.

## Preconditions
- **Renunciation first.** If the Indian-origin spouse/parent ever held an Indian passport, the checklist requires their **surrender/renunciation certificate** as the proof of Indian origin (old Indian passport copy + certificate), or their **OCI card** copy. No certificate and no OCI → the Indian-origin person applies for renunciation (`surrender.md`) before this visa can be filed. For passports surrendered after May 2010 both the cancelled passport and the certificate are needed.
- If the **applicant** ever held an Indian passport, their own renunciation proof is mandatory as well.
- Applicant's passport valid ≥ 6 months with **≥ 2 blank pages**. The **original passport is submitted** (unlike OCI) and is away for the processing period.
- Photo: **2x2 inch, glossy, white background** (the X-visa spec — opposite of the OCI pale background); digital JPEG **10–300 KB**, plain light/white background, no glasses. One photo session cannot serve both an OCI and a visa application; see `photo-specs.md`.
- Online form on indianvisaonline.gov.in must be filled in the current year and submitted within 180 days; nothing editable after submission.
- Register on the VFS site before mailing (mandatory) and ship the complete application within **30 days** of registration.
- **Additional Particulars form (Annexure A) is mandatory for everyone, including minors** — the application is held without it.
- Minor applicant: both parents sign the form and the notarized **Parental Authorization (Annexure B)**; child signs or gives a thumb impression.

## Checklist (intake order, Sep 2025 checklist)
| # | Document | Form |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Government online visa application form, printed and signed (minor: both parents sign + child's signature/thumb) | Original |
| 2 | 2x2 glossy photo affixed (not stapled), identical to the uploaded 10–300 KB JPEG | Original |
| 3 | VFS application / payment confirmation | Original |
| 4 | Proof of US address matching the form: state ID, DL, utility ≤ 3 months, lease, mortgage, tax return (students: college letter; living with family: relative's proof + undertaking) | Copy |
| 5 | US legal status if non-US passport: green card, visa, EAD, I-797A, or extension receipt | Copy |
| 6 | **Additional Particulars form — Annexure A** | Original |
| 7 | **Passport** (≥ 6 months, ≥ 2 blank pages) + photocopy of bio pages. Dual nationals holding a Pakistani passport apply on it and submit both passports in original. | Original + copy |
| 8 | Proof of renunciation, if the applicant ever held an Indian passport (certificate; plus cancelled passport if surrendered after May 2010) | Copy |
| 9 | **Proof of Indian origin — by relationship:** | Copies |
|   | Spouse of an Indian national → marriage certificate + spouse's Indian passport copy | |
|   | Spouse of a PIO / OCI holder → marriage certificate + (spouse's old Indian passport **and** surrender/renunciation certificate) **or** spouse's OCI card | |
|   | Child of an Indian national → birth certificate + either parent's Indian passport + that parent's US legal status | |
|   | Child of a PIO / OCI holder → birth certificate + (parent's old Indian passport **and** renunciation certificate) **or** either parent's OCI card | |
| 10 | Minor: Parental Authorization (Annexure B) both parents, notarized; birth certificate; both parents' passports; parents' marriage certificate; parents' US status if non-US nationals. Signatures must match across form, annexure and passports. | Original + copies |
| 11 | Proof of property in India, if any, in the applicant's name with RBI permission | Copy, condition-based |
| 12 | Annexure C — Pakistani nationals only (jurisdiction-specific form from the one-pager) | Original |
| 13 | Checklist printed, signed, enclosed as cover | Original |

The checklist does not require the marriage certificate to be apostilled (the OCI one does). Non-English documents: certified translation. A religious marriage certificate is not excluded here, but the consulate can ask for the civil one; bring the civil certificate.

## Submission
- By post after VFS registration (own prepaid label entered during registration and enclosed; schedule pickup when notified), or in person by appointment at the jurisdictional VFS centre (`centres.md`).
- Passport returns by your label or VFS courier (+$18). No biometrics mentioned on any fetched US page.

## In India
An X-visa holder staying over 180 days must register with the FRRO/FRO within 14 days of arrival (MHA rule; UNVERIFIED on the fetched VFS pages, which are silent). Flag it; the visa sticker usually states it.

## After arrival — converting to OCI
Once the marriage passes two years, file OCI (`oci-spouse.md`). The X visa stays usable until its expiry; once the e-OCI is granted, travel on it.
