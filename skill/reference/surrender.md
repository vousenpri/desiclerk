# Surrender of Indian passport / Renunciation certificate (USA, via VFS)

Verified 2026-10-27 against the VFS USA one-pager and the adult with-passport checklist dated 2026-10-20.

## Who
Anyone who ever held an Indian passport and acquired another nationality. Required before fresh OCI if the passport was surrendered after 31 May 2010. A plain "cancelled" stamp is not renunciation; the stamp must say cancelled on acquiring foreign nationality.

## Fee (one-pager, 2026-10-27)
| Item | USD |
|---|---|
| Surrender / renunciation fee | 40 |
| ICWF | 3 |
| VFS service charge | 19 |
| **Total** | **62** |
- Card (Visa/Mastercard only) adds 3.75% convenience charge.
- Money order / cashier's check payable to "VFS Services (USA) Inc." Personal checks refused.
- No longer split by naturalization date (older PDFs that say "before / after 1 June 2010" are stale; the split now only decides which *checklist* and whether a Declaration vs Surrender certificate is issued).
- Refund on rejection: consular + ICWF only.

## Preconditions (before any form)
- VFS online registration must be completed first; physical application without it is returned unprocessed.
- Government form: `https://embassy.passportindia.gov.in/` — fill, submit, print in **portrait**, single-sided. The printed form must show the photo (see gotchas: Chrome).
- Online form must be from the current year and under 320 days old at submission.
- Two 2x2 inch colour photos, **white background only**, photographic paper, under 6 months old. (This is the opposite of the OCI upload spec. Do not reuse.)
- Nothing handwritten. No staples. No double-sided printing.

## Checklist — adult, possessing most recent Indian passport
In intake order, as the checklist lists them:
1. Government application form — original printout (signed).
2. 2 photos (2x2, white bg) — original.
3. **Two** duly filled and signed Surrender of Indian Passport Certificate forms — original (fillable PDF on VFS site).
4. Proof of address — copy. One of: state ID, driver's licence (do not crop), utility bill ≤3 months, lease, mortgage, tax return, dorm letter. PO Box not accepted. Expired documents not accepted. Living with relatives → their notarized undertaking + their address proof.
5. US passport — copy of info page + last two amendment pages.
6. Affidavit of naturalization after expiry of Indian passport — original, notarized. **Only if** naturalized after the Indian passport expired ("gap letter").
7. Name change document — copy. **Only if** name differs in any way from Indian passport: marriage certificate, court order, notarized affidavit (middle initial added), or naturalization endorsement. Certificate will carry the name as on the Indian passport.
8. Most recent Indian passport — **original**, plus copy of first three and last two pages.
9. Naturalization certificate — copy. All nationalities ever held if more than one. Naturalized through parents → parents' certificates + notarized statement by both parents.
10. Parental authorisation — minors only.
11. Sworn affidavit by parents — only if naturalized through parents.
12. Fee payment confirmation / courier label (UPS or USPS only, no expiry; FedEx refused). Return address must match address proof.
13. The checklist itself, printed and signed, as cover page.

## Variants
- **Without passport (lost):** Declaration of Renunciation instead of Surrender certificate; affidavit of loss of passport (notarized); any two proofs of Indian origin (voter ID, SSC/board certificate, birth certificate, parent's Indian passport). Decision on acceptability is the consular officer's.
- **Minor:** both parents sign; thumb impression under 5; parental authorisation + sworn affidavit by parents.
- **Declaration of Renunciation** (no passport) is issued only if 10 years have passed since naturalization.

## Timing
- 7–21 working days after reaching the consulate.
- Incomplete application: on hold; missing documents due within 7 days of the deficiency email; abandoned and returned after 20–21 days, fees partly refunded, process restarts.

## Envelope
"SURRENDER APPLICATION WITH PASSPORT" (or "SURRENDER DECLARATION – WITHOUT PASSPORT"). One application per envelope.

## Not required (common generic-agent error)
- A notarized "personal appearance" or signature-change affidavit is **not** on the VFS checklist. See `gotchas.md` for the New York vs Edison observation.
