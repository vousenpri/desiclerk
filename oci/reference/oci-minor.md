# Fresh OCI — minor born in the USA (never held an Indian passport), parent Indian citizen / former Indian citizen / OCI holder

Verified 2026-10-27 against:
- VFS USA one-pager, OCI services: https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/ (fees, eligibility, courier)
- VFS checklist "Minor – New – Foreign national by birth and never held Indian passport" (Sep 2026): https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/pdf/Minor-New-Foreign-national-by-birth-and-never-held-Indian-passport-minor-sep-2026.pdf
- VFS Parental Authorization form: https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/oci-services/pdf/parental-authorization.pdf
- CGI Chicago e-OCI notice (2026-07-09): https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/section/news/e-oci-electronic-overseas-citizen-of-india-card/

A minor who **did** hold an Indian passport uses a different checklist (`Minor-Document-Checklist-for-Foreign-national-previously-Indian-passport-holder-minor-sep-2026.pdf`) and must surrender it first. This file is for the US-born child only.

## Eligibility (one-pager wording)
- Minor child of a person eligible for OCI (former Indian citizen / person of Indian origin / OCI holder), **or**
- minor child whose both parents, or one parent, is an Indian citizen.
- Not eligible if any parent / grandparent / great-grandparent is or was a citizen of Pakistan or Bangladesh (consulate pages; not on the VFS checklist).

## Preconditions (before opening ociservices.gov.in)
- **No physical card is issued.** From 8 July 2026 all new applicants get an e-OCI only, downloaded once status reads "Granted". Do not plan travel around a card in the mail.
- Child's US passport valid **≥ 9 months** at submission (this checklist; the adult former-Indian checklist says 6). Renew first if short.
- Birth certificate must be the **state/county-issued** long form naming **both parents** with their date of birth and nationality. Hospital certificates are refused. The Sep 2026 checklist, verbatim: "If the applicant was born outside of India, the birth certificate must either be apostilled by the relevant foreign government or attested by the appropriate foreign mission." A US-born child is born outside India, so **the apostille is required**, not optional. "Do not send original apostilled documents to VFS or Consulate office. We require self-attested copies of the apostilled documents instead." NYC chain: NYC Health long form + letter of exemplification (45 Lafayette St Rm 133 by appointment, or VitalChek with reason Apostille/Authentication, $15) → NY County Clerk, 60 Centre St Rm 141B, signature authentication ($3) → NY Dept of State, 123 William St, apostille ($10). Re-verified 2026-10-28.
- Parents' marriage certificate must be a **civil registrar** certificate. Religious/temple/church certificates are refused. Married in India → Marriage Registrar certificate. Married in the US or elsewhere outside India → **apostilled** by the issuing state (VFS: marriage certificate issued outside India must be apostilled or attested). Send a self-attested copy of the apostilled original. See `apostille.md`.
- Any name difference for the child or for either parent across documents → notarized name-change affidavit or court order, and "previous name" filled in on the form.
- Photo and signature/thumb JPEGs made to spec first (see `photo-specs.md`). Photo ≤ 6 months old; print and upload identical.
- **Signature rule by age:** under 5 → upload the child's **left thumb impression**; 5 and over → the child's own signature in blue/black ink (no e-signature). Parents must **not** sign in the applicant's signature box; a parent signs **Part B** only.
- Both parents available to sign the Parental Authorization before a notary (exceptions below).
- All supporting documents scanned to PDF, ≤ 1000 KB each (older VFS/Govt text says 500 KB; target 500), one file per portal category. Missing uploads = returned unprocessed.
- Form expires 90 days after completion; nothing editable after submission.

## Parental Authorization — who signs
- Default: **both** parents, before a notary, with a copy of each parent's ID showing signature and the child's birth certificate attached.
- One parent may sign only with: a court order granting that parent sole legal custody (translated if not English), **or** the other parent's death certificate, **or** a single-parent adoption document.
- Signature mismatch between the Authorization, the form and the parents' passports is a listed cause of delay/rejection.

## Listed rejection causes (same list as the adult checklist)
1. Poor-quality uploaded photo or signature/thumb.
2. Not all supporting documents uploaded.
3. Wrong passport number or date of issue.
4. Wrong name / place of birth / date of birth.
5. **Parents' full names missing or not matching their current legal names** (the usual minor-specific failure: mother's maiden name on the birth certificate vs married name on her passport → explain with the marriage certificate and enter both).
6. Marital status of parents not entered.
7. Wrong phone or email.
8. **Part B not signed by a parent.**
9. Previous name not entered where a name changed.
10. Incomplete postal address (ZIP).
11. Native address proof missing (non-US-national parents).

## Timing
Consulate figures: CGI Chicago lists new OCI at 5–6 weeks from consular registration, subject to MHA clearance. VFS states none. Plan 8–10 weeks end to end (see `timing.md`). e-OCI appears on the portal at "Granted"; nothing is mailed back except the documents you sent.

## After grant — maintenance for a minor
Each new passport, at any age → free portal update within 3 months, photo under 30 days old (`oci-maintenance.md`). No re-issue at 20 any more (CGI Chicago, Oct 2026).
