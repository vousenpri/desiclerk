# OCI maintenance — what to do after a new passport

Current rule verified 2026-10-30 against CGI Chicago, "OCI card updation; reissuance of OCI cards discontinued", page last updated 27 Oct 2026: https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/page/oci-card-updation-reissuance-of-oci-cards-discontinued/ . CGI San Francisco advisory of 2026-06-16 says the same on the late fee. The 27 Oct date is when Chicago's page was last edited, not necessarily when the policy began; the policy was in force by then.

## The rule (one line)
**Every new passport, any age → update the passport particulars on the OCI portal within 3 months. Free. No card is issued. Late costs $25 + $3 + $19.**

## What this replaced
Until 2026 the MHA rule was: re-issue the card once after the first passport issued after age 20; upload each new passport under 20 and once after 50; nothing in between. Chicago now states that "the provision for reissuance of Physical OCI Cards has been discontinued" and "the earlier requirement of mandatory OCI Card reissuance upon issuance of a new passport is no longer applicable." **The VFS one-pager and the Embassy page (indianembassyusa.gov.in/pages/NjI) still show the old text.** When a user quotes the old rule, explain that it was superseded, point to the Chicago page, and recommend the current rule: it is free and cannot be wrong in the user's disfavour. Re-check quarterly.

## How to do the update
- ociservices.gov.in → OCI Miscellaneous Services → passport update. Upload: new passport bio page (PDF) + a photo **taken within the last 30 days** (JPEG, `photo-specs.md`). Nothing is posted to VFS; no visit.
- Within 3 months of the passport's issue date. Acknowledgement by email; records update in about 5 weeks; nothing is mailed back.
- After 3 months: the same portal form, but a fee of $25 consular + $3 ICWF + $19 VFS, paid through the VFS centre serving the user's consulate. Card +3.75%.
- Travel in the meantime: allowed on the existing e-OCI/card bearing the old passport number, carrying the new passport, during and after the window.

## What still goes through VFS as "Misc Services"
- Change of name, father's name, nationality (not to Pakistan/Bangladesh): $25 + $3 + $19.
- Lost or damaged OCI: $100 + $3 + $19 (`oci-reissue.md`; with e-OCI the "card" is a PDF, confirm with the consulate what, if anything, is re-issued).
- Errors on the OCI: $25 + $3 + $19.
- Renunciation of OCI: $25 + $3 + $19 (VFS checklists, Aug 2025).

## Foreign spouse
Every new passport → update, plus a declaration that the marriage subsists and copies of the Indian/OCI spouse's passport and OCI. Spouse OCI is valid 5 years; its expiry is a re-issue trigger (CGI Chicago PDF). See `oci-spouse.md`.

## Household triggers (what the agent puts on the calendar)
For each family member record: date of birth, passport issue and expiry, OCI number and the passport number on it, last update date. Then:
- Any passport renewed (child every 5 years, adult every 10) → `Desi Clerk · Upload new passport to OCI by` = issue date + 3 months. Photo under 30 days old at upload.
- Name change → Misc Services re-issue.
- Foreign spouse passport renewed → update + marriage declaration.
- Anyone joining a foreign military or police → must renounce OCI.
