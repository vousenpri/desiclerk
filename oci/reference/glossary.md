# Glossary — say it in plain words

The agent uses the left column with the user unless the user uses the term first. The right column is what the form or the desk calls it.

| Say this | The official word | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Give back your Indian passport | Surrender / renunciation | Cancelling Indian citizenship formally after becoming a US citizen. Mandatory for naturalizations on/after 1 Jun 2010. |
| The certificate | Surrender certificate / Renunciation certificate | The paper the consulate returns with the cancelled passport. OCI cannot be filed without it. |
| OCI | Overseas Citizen of India | Lifelong visa-like status for former Indians and their descendants. Not citizenship. Since 8 July 2026 it is a PDF (e-OCI), not a card. |
| An email saying something is missing | Deficiency / on hold / additional documents | Consulate request; 7 days to answer. |
| A notarized letter saying both names are you | One-and-the-same-person affidavit / name-change affidavit | Sworn statement, signed before a US notary, listing every version of your name. |
| A copy you've signed | Self-attested copy | Photocopy with your signature and date on it. VFS wants copies, never originals, of apostilled papers. |
| Notarised by a government | Apostille | A state's certificate that a public record's signature is real, so another country accepts it. |
| The long form | Long-form birth certificate with letter of exemplification | The full record naming both parents; the only version a state will apostille. |
| Promise form | Undertaking | A VFS form you sign promising something (e.g. you've given up your Indian voter card). |
| Letter that lets them fix typos | Consent letter | Authorises the consulate to correct small errors instead of returning the file. |
| The parents' permission form | Parental authorization | Both parents sign before a notary, in person, for a child's OCI. |
| The desk | VFS centre / ICAC | The private company (VFS Global) counter that receives applications for the consulate. |
| The consulate | Consulate General of India / Embassy | The government office that decides. You never deal with it directly for these. |
| Passport Seva | mportal.passportindia.gov.in | Government site where the surrender form is filled. |
| OCI portal | ociservices.gov.in | Government site where the OCI form is filled and files uploaded. |
| Upload file | Digital photo / signature JPEG | 600 × 600 JPEG under 30 KB (photo); 600 × 200 under 30 KB (signature or thumbprint). |
| Print file | 2 × 2 photograph | The physical photo stuck on the printed form; must be the same picture as the upload. |
| Money order | Banker's draft / cashier's check | Paper payment to "VFS Services (USA) Inc."; avoids the 3.75% card fee. |
| Part B | Part B of the OCI form | The signature block on the printed OCI form; a parent signs it for a child. |
| Thumbprint | Thumb impression | Replaces the signature for a child under 5. Left thumb. |
