## 0. The three triggers (use these labels throughout)

| Label | Legal source | Test | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1: FEMA non-resident** ("person resident outside India", FEMA s.2(w)) | FEMA 1999 s.2(v)(i)(A): a person who has left India "for or on taking up employment outside India", for business/vocation, or "for any other purpose, in such circumstances as would indicate his intention to stay outside India for an uncertain period" is excluded from "person resident in India" regardless of the 182-day count. Source: Act text at https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1988/1/a199942.pdf (official, outside allowlist; linked from https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/Act.aspx). RBI FAQ restating lead-in: https://rbi.org.in/commonman/english/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=357 (dated 16-Jan-2025). | Intention-based; takes effect on the date of departure for employment/indefinite stay. | Day the person leaves India for good (or for a US job). Drives: NRO redesignation, NRE eligibility, PIS/demat, property, repatriation. |
| **T2: Income-tax non-resident** (Income-tax Act s.6) | Resident if (a) 182+ days in India in the FY, or (b) 60+ days in FY and 365+ days in preceding 4 FYs. For an Indian citizen leaving India "for the purpose of employment outside India" or as crew, and for an Indian citizen/PIO *visiting* India, the 60-day limb becomes 182 days; it becomes 120 days if India-source income (excluding foreign-source income) exceeds Rs 15 lakh. Deemed resident (s.6(1A)): Indian citizen with India-source income > Rs 15 lakh who is "not liable to tax in any country or jurisdiction by reason of his domicile or residence". RNOR: non-resident in 9 of preceding 10 FYs, or ≤729 days in India in preceding 7 FYs (and the 120–181 day case above). Sources: https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Documents/residential-status.htm ; https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/provisions-of-income-tax-law-and-fema-useful-for-non-residents (reflects Finance Act 2026). | Day-count, assessed per Indian FY (1 Apr–31 Mar). | Usually the first full FY after departure. Drives: ITR filing, TDS rates, Aadhaar–PAN exemption. |
| **T3: Naturalized as US citizen** | Citizenship Act 1955 s.9 (automatic termination on voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship; "Persons of Indian Origin are NOT allowed to hold DUAL Citizenship", https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/extra?id=76). | Date on US naturalization certificate. | Drives: passport surrender, renunciation certificate, voter-roll ineligibility, OCI eligibility, NPS closure. |
| **T3a: Acquired OCI** | Citizenship Act 1955 s.7A–7D, MHA notification 04-Mar-2021 (https://hciottawa.gov.in/whatsnew?id=55). | Date on OCI card. | Drives: OCI re-issue/upload calendar, OCI activity restrictions, NPS continuation. |

Household rule of thumb: T1 and T2 can occur years before T3; a person can be T1/T2 non-resident while still an Indian citizen (NRI). After T3 the person is a foreign national; with T3a they are an OCI cardholder and are treated "at par with NRIs" for FEMA/economic purposes, with listed exceptions.

## 1. Indian bank accounts, deposits, demat, PPF, NPS, EPF

### 1.1 Resident savings account → NRO (mandatory)

- **Trigger:** T1 (FEMA non-resident).
- **Rule (verbatim):** "when a resident Indian becomes a person resident outside India, his existing resident account should be designated as NRO account." RBI Master Direction – Deposits and Accounts, FED Master Direction No. 14/2015-16, Part II para 6.10. https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasterDirections.aspx?id=10198 (issued 01-Jan-2016, updated as on 02-Sep-2026; fetched 2026-10-27).
- **Eligibility test:** Any individual who has left India per FEMA s.2(v)(i)(A). Not tied to citizenship: applies equally to NRIs and (after T3/T3a) to OCI/PIO.
- **Deadline/window:** None stated in the Master Direction. The direction uses "should be designated"; no grace period and no explicit duty-to-inform clause was found (para 6.10 is phrased as a bank obligation; the account holder's declaration is referenced only in the "pending dues" context, para 6.12). UNVERIFIED: any statutory time limit for the customer to notify the bank.
- **Consequence of non-compliance:** UNVERIFIED on official pages (FEMA s.13 contravention penalties are general; no RBI page fetched states a specific penalty for continuing to operate a resident account as a non-resident).
- **Related: NRO repatriation.** Para 6.8: NRO balances "cannot be repatriated abroad except by NRIs and PIOs up to USD 1 million" subject to Remittance of Assets Regulations 2016; current income (rent, dividend, pension, interest) remittable without the cap. The FEMA (Deposit) Regulations 2016 circular (A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 67, 05-May-2016) para 5(C)(iii) states "up to USD 1 million per financial year". https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10382
- **Classification:**
  - *Legally overdue:* person is T1 non-resident AND still holds an account designated "resident" (savings/current).
  - *Action recommended:* account already NRO but joint holder/nominee is resident; or NRE/FCNR funds sit in NRO (loses repatriability/tax-free interest).
  - *Information missing:* date of departure; whether departure was for employment/indefinite stay (T1 test); list of Indian accounts and current designation per bank.

### 1.2 NRE account (optional, eligibility only)

- **Trigger:** T1.
- **Eligibility:** "Non-resident Indians (NRIs) and Person of Indian Origin (PIOs) are permitted to open and maintain these accounts" (MD 14, Part II para 4.1). OCI cardholders qualify as PIO under FEMA definitions. Joint with resident relative on "former or survivor" basis permitted (Deposit Regs para 5(A)(x)).
- **On return to India:** NRE accounts "should be designated as resident accounts" or transferred to RFC "immediately upon the return of the account holder to India for taking up employment or on change in the residential status" (para 4.9).
- **Classification:** Never "legally overdue" (optional product). *Action recommended* if person wants repatriable/Indian-tax-free rupee deposits. *Information missing:* none beyond T1 status and citizenship/OCI status.

### 1.3 Existing resident fixed deposits

- Master Direction para 6.10 applies to "existing resident account" without distinguishing savings from term deposits; FDs are redesignated NRO (non-repatriable principal). No separate FD rule found. UNVERIFIED: whether banks must break/re-book resident FDs vs. relabel to maturity.
- **Classification:** same as 1.1.

### 1.4 Demat / shares / mutual funds acquired as resident

- **Trigger:** T1.
- **Rule:** Under FEMA 20 Schedule 4 (non-repatriation basis), NRI investment is "deemed to be domestic investment at par with the investment made by residents"; sale proceeds "shall be credited only to NRO account irrespective of the type of account" used for purchase; proceeds of securities "purchased while resident in India" do not go to NRE (PIS). Fresh exchange purchases on repatriation basis require PIS through "a branch designated by an Authorised Dealer" (Schedule 3; 5% per-NRI / 10% aggregate caps). Existing NRO (PIS) accounts "may be re-designated as NRO account". Source: FEMA.361/2016-RB, 15-Feb-2016, https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_FemaNotifications.aspx?Id=10288. NOTE: FEMA 20 has since been superseded by the FEM (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules 2019 (Ministry of Finance); substance carried over; UNVERIFIED on allowlisted pages — the NDI Rules were not fetched.
- **Deadline:** none stated.
- **Consequence:** UNVERIFIED.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue* if T1 person is still trading through a resident demat/trading account with resident bank mandate. *Action recommended* if holdings are dormant but KYC still shows resident. *Information missing:* demat/MF folios exist? broker KYC status; whether person wants to trade post-T1.

### 1.5 PPF

- UNVERIFIED on allowlisted sources: the PPF Scheme 2019 (Ministry of Finance, DEA) governs; nsiindia.gov.in blocked fetch (robots). Leads only (not citable): NRIs cannot open new PPF; existing account may continue to maturity on non-repatriation basis; no extension after maturity. Classify as *information missing* until the scheme text is read.

### 1.6 NPS

- **Trigger:** T3 (renunciation/loss of Indian citizenship) without T3a.
- **Rule:** PFRDA circular PFRDA/2025/2/REG-EXIT/01 dated 21-Apr-2025, titled "Settlement of corpus / closure of NPS account in case NPS subscriber renounces Indian citizenship and does not hold OCI card". https://pfrda.org.in/web/pfrda/w/regulatory-framework/circulars/active/settlement-of-corpus-closure-of-nps-account-in-case-nps-subscriber-renounces-indian-citizenship-and-does-not-hold-oci-card-1 (official, outside allowlist; PDF body did not render — only title/number/date verified). Eligibility page: "Must be an Indian Citizen (resident or non-resident) or an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)"; "Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) are not eligible". https://pfrda.org.in/web/pfrda/schemes/national-pension-system/nps-for-all-citizen-models
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue* if T3 occurred, no OCI held, and NPS account still open (closure is mandated by title; terms UNVERIFIED). *Action recommended* if OCI held (continuation permitted; check KYC). *Information missing:* NPS PRAN exists? OCI held?

### 1.7 EPF

- UNVERIFIED: no epfindia.gov.in page found via search. Lead only: final settlement permissible on "migrating abroad for permanent settlement" (Form 19). Classify *information missing*.

## 2. US-side reporting (applies from the first year the person is a "US person": green card / substantial presence / citizenship — i.e., well before T3)

### 2.1 FBAR (FinCEN Form 114)

- **Trigger:** Being a "U.S. person" (citizen, resident alien, domestic entity) with financial interest in or signature authority over foreign accounts whose aggregate value "exceeded $10,000 at any time during the calendar year." Sources: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar (reviewed 30-Jul-2026); https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts.
- **Eligibility test:** US person status under US tax law (not Indian status). NRO/NRE/FCNR accounts, Indian demat, Indian mutual funds, PPF, and insurance with cash value count toward the aggregate.
- **Deadline:** April 15 following the calendar year; "automatic extension to October 15" without request. Filed electronically through FinCEN BSA E-Filing, not with the tax return. Keep records 5 years.
- **Consequence:** Civil penalty maxima (inflation-adjusted, effective 17-Jan-2025): non-willful $16,536 per violation (31 U.S.C. 5321(a)(5)(B)(i)); willful $165,353 (5321(a)(5)(C)(i)(I)) or greater-of-50%-of-balance (statutory; the 50% alternative was not visible in the fetched table). Source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-17/html/2025-01374.htm (FinCEN rule, official, outside allowlist). Criminal penalties possible.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue* if US person, aggregate > $10,000 in any prior calendar year, and no FBAR filed by Oct 15 of the following year. *Action recommended* if aggregate hovers near $10,000 or if delinquent filings should go through IRS delinquent-FBAR procedures. *Information missing:* year US-person status began; peak aggregate balance per calendar year across all Indian accounts (including accounts where the person is joint holder or POA/signatory, e.g., parents' accounts).

### 2.2 FATCA Form 8938

- **Trigger:** "Specified individual" (US citizen, resident alien) holding specified foreign financial assets above threshold.
- **Thresholds (verbatim, IRS):** Living in US — unmarried or MFS: > $50,000 on last day OR > $75,000 at any time; MFJ: > $100,000 last day OR > $150,000 any time. Living abroad — unmarried: $200,000 / $300,000; MFJ: $400,000 / $600,000. Source: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/summary-of-fatca-reporting-for-us-taxpayers (reviewed 19-Sep-2026).
- **Deadline:** "due with your annual income tax return" (so Apr 15 / Oct 15 with extension).
- **Consequence:** $10,000 failure-to-file; up to $50,000 additional for continued failure after IRS notice; 40% penalty on understatement attributable to undisclosed assets.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue* if thresholds exceeded in a filed year and no 8938 attached. *Information missing:* filing status; year-end and peak values of Indian financial assets (bank, demat, MF, insurance cash value, PPF, NPS, EPF).

### 2.3 Form 3520 (gifts/bequests from India)

- **Trigger:** US person receives in a tax year (a) > $100,000 in aggregate gifts/bequests from a nonresident alien individual or foreign estate (aggregating related donors), or (b) > $20,116 (2025) / $20,573 (2026) from foreign corporations/partnerships. Source: https://www.irs.gov/node/6195 ("Gifts from foreign person", reviewed 17-Apr-2026); instructions https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3520 (Rev. 12/2025).
- **Eligibility:** Typical household case: parents in India gift money or the household inherits Indian property (bequest). Inherited Indian immovable property is reportable by value even if no cash moved.
- **Deadline:** 15th day of 4th month after tax year (Apr 15); extended with the income-tax extension to no later than the 15th day of the 10th month (Oct 15). June 15 for US persons living abroad.
- **Consequence:** IRC 6039F(c): 5% of the gift per month unreported, capped at 25%, absent reasonable cause.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue* if a qualifying gift/inheritance occurred in a closed year with no 3520. *Information missing:* any gift/inheritance from India by year and donor; donor relationship (aggregation).

### 2.4 PFIC (Indian mutual funds, ULIPs) — note only

- IRS definition: a foreign corporation is a PFIC if "75% or more of the corporation's gross income for its tax year is passive income" or "at least 50% of the average percentage of assets" are passive. Form 8621 required per PFIC per year, with a Part I exception if aggregate PFIC stock ≤ $25,000 ($50,000 MFJ) on last day and no excess distribution/disposition. Source: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8621 (Rev. 12/2025). UNVERIFIED: an IRS page stating in terms that Indian mutual funds are PFICs (treatment follows from the definition; the instructions fetched do not name mutual funds).
- **Classification:** *Action recommended* whenever the household holds Indian mutual funds/ULIPs as a US person; never "legally overdue" without a tax computation. *Information missing:* list of Indian MF/ULIP holdings, acquisition dates, distributions/redemptions by year.

## 3. PAN

### 3.1 Residential status / address in PAN record

- **Trigger:** T2 (and, practically, T3: citizenship field).
- **Rule:** The Department's PAN-correction page: any change to the details recorded at the time of obtaining PAN must be reported through "Request For New PAN Card Or/ And Changes or Correction in PAN Data" (Protean/UTIITSL/online). https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/how-to-apply-for-pan-correction- . NRI PAN guidance (Form 49A; proof of address may be overseas bank statement or NRE statement): https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Documents/pan-nri.htm . Statutory basis s.139A(5)(d) (intimate AO of change of address) — page fetched blank; UNVERIFIED verbatim.
- **Deadline:** none stated.
- **Consequence:** none stated on official pages. Practical consequence: Aadhaar–PAN exemption (3.2) depends on the Department recognising non-resident status; TDS/26AS mismatches.
- **Classification:** *Action recommended* (not "legally overdue") once T2 is met: update address/status so the PAN is flagged non-resident. *Information missing:* current PAN address and status flag; whether the e-filing profile shows "Non-Resident".

### 3.2 Aadhaar–PAN linking (s.139AA) — exemption for non-residents

- **Trigger:** Linking mandate applies to persons eligible for Aadhaar; exemption for "a non-resident as per the Income-tax Act, 1961" (T2), persons "not a citizen of India" (T3), persons aged 80+, residents of Assam/J&K/Meghalaya. Notification No. 37/2017 dated 11-May-2017. Source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/e-filing-link-aadhaar-faq (Department e-filing FAQ; cites Circular 03/2023 of 28-Mar-2023).
- **Consequence of non-linking (for those not exempt):** PAN inoperative from 01-Jul-2023; no refunds, no interest on refunds for inoperative period; higher TDS under s.206AA and TCS under s.206CC; Rs 1,000 fee to reactivate.
- **Classification:**
  - *Legally overdue:* person was T2-resident for a FY after 2017, held both PAN and Aadhaar, and PAN shows inoperative. (Household edge case: a member who left India mid-year and was still "resident" for that FY.)
  - *Action recommended:* person is T2 non-resident/T3 but PAN database still shows resident and PAN has gone inoperative — file the status update (3.1) and get the inoperative flag lifted.
  - *Information missing:* PAN operative status; Aadhaar held?; T2 status for each FY since 2017-18.

## 4. Aadhaar

- **Eligibility:** Aadhaar Act 2016 s.3: only a "resident" (182 days in preceding 12 months) is entitled. UIDAI NRI FAQ: for NRIs "Resided in India for at least 182 days is not applicable for NRI"; "valid Indian passport is mandatory as Proof of Identity"; email mandatory. https://uidai.gov.in/contact-support/have-any-question/291-faqs/your-aadhaar/nri-aadhaar.html and https://uidai.gov.in/en/291-faqs/your-aadhaar/nri-aadhaar/12170-what-is-the-process-for-aadhaar-enrolment-for-children-of-nris.html . Mission pages (2017–18, pre-amendment, now partly superseded for NRIs) state OCI cardholders may enrol only after 182 days' stay in the preceding 12 months with an Indian address: https://hciabuja.gov.in/pages/MTQz ; https://www.cgidubai.gov.in/section/press-releases/aadhaar-requirements/ (03-Jan-2018). The Abuja page also records DoT exempting NRIs/PIOs/OCIs from Aadhaar–mobile linking (letter of 01-12-2017).
- **Trigger for action:** T1/T2 (leaving) — none found. T3 (foreign citizenship) — no official page found requiring surrender or deactivation of Aadhaar. **UNVERIFIED: any obligation to surrender, update, or deactivate Aadhaar on leaving India or on naturalization.**
- **Consequence:** none found.
- **Classification:** Never "legally overdue" on current evidence. *Action recommended:* keep Aadhaar demographic data (address, mobile) current if it is used for Indian bank/UPI/e-KYC; note that OCI holders without 182 days' stay cannot newly enrol. *Information missing:* Aadhaar held?; whether any Indian service (bank, SIM, DigiLocker) depends on it.

## 5. Voter ID / electoral roll

- **Overseas elector (still Indian citizen, T1 but not T3):** RPA 1950 s.20A: a citizen of India absent from ordinary residence in India for employment, education or otherwise may register as an "Overseas elector" in the constituency of the address in the passport, via Form 6A (online at voterportal.eci.gov.in or at Missions), provided the name is not already on a roll and the person "has not acquired the citizenship of any other country". No EPIC is issued; vote in person on production of original passport. Sources: CGI San Francisco press release 18-Mar-2019 https://www.cgisf.gov.in/docs/15537531212222.pdf ; CGI Chicago (SVEEP/ECI text) https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/page/nri-voter-enrollment-process/ . A general elector who moves abroad and applies as overseas elector must surrender the EPIC.
- **Foreign citizenship (T3):** Eligibility ends ("has not acquired the citizenship of any other country"). On OCI application: "Applicants who are holding Voter ID and Ration Card must surrender and provide an undertaking along with the OCI application." https://indembassybern.gov.in/page/general-instructions-for-new-oci-registration (also https://www.indembassybern.gov.in/page/revised-guidelines-on-re-issue-of-oci-cards). UNVERIFIED: an ECI page prescribing a deletion form (Form 7) or penalty for a non-citizen remaining on the roll; eci.gov.in returned no indexable page.
- **Deadline:** Surrender is a precondition of the OCI application (so: at OCI filing). No standalone deadline found.
- **Consequence:** UNVERIFIED (RPA 1950 s.16/s.31 false-declaration provisions are the lead; not fetched).
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue:* T3 person still enrolled as general or overseas elector (ineligible), especially if applying for OCI. *Action recommended:* T1-only member holding a general-roll EPIC while living abroad (should convert to overseas elector or request deletion). *Information missing:* EPIC held?; current roll status (check voters.eci.gov.in); OCI applied/held?

## 6. Indian passport surrender / renunciation

- **Trigger:** T3. Indian citizenship ceases automatically on voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship; "Persons of Indian Origin are NOT allowed to hold DUAL Citizenship" and must surrender the Indian passport. https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/extra?id=76 . Applies to persons who acquired foreign nationality "on or after June 01, 2010" (https://cgitoronto.gov.in/page/general-information-surrender, updated 29-10-2024); earlier naturalisations are outside the stated requirement (they still need proof of cancellation for OCI/visa, UNVERIFIED on US-mission page).
- **Eligibility:** Every former Indian passport holder who naturalised, including minors (separate VFS checklists for minor with/without passport).
- **Window:** Toronto: surrender "at the earliest"/"immediately after acquiring that citizenship". The penalty schedule uses a 3-year retention threshold (below). CGI SF FAQ: surrender certificate obtainable "within 3 years of acquisition of foreign citizenship" (https://cgisf.gov.in/page/FAQs-R) — read as the no-retention-penalty window, not a bar to later surrender.
- **Fees (USA, via VFS):** Surrender fee $40 + ICWF $3 + VFS $19 (card 3.75%); processing 7–21 working days after receipt at mission. https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/renunciation-of-indian-citizenship/index.html ; CGI Chicago (updated 27-Oct-2026): "US$ 40/- plus US$ 3/- ICWF plus VFS service fee" https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/page/renunciation-of-indian-citizenship-certificate-declaration-of-renunciation-of-citizenship-of-india/ . (Embassy page extra?id=76 shows an older $25 figure; treat $40 as current.) Renunciation (for a *current* Indian citizen renouncing before/at naturalisation) is a separate application at indiancitizenshiponline.nic.in; fee not stated on US pages (Pretoria quotes INR 8,000-equivalent).
- **Penalty schedule (Passports Act 1967 s.12(1A)); as published by Embassy of India, Bern** https://indembassybern.gov.in/page/passport-pentalty (no date on page; CHF conversions shown there omitted):
  1. Not surrendered ≤3 years, not used for travel: no penalty.
  2. ≤3 years, travelled once on Indian passport after naturalisation: Rs 10,000.
  3. ≤3 years, travelled more than once: Rs 10,000 per trip, cap Rs 50,000.
  4. Retained >3 years and used for travel: Rs 10,000 for retention + Rs 10,000 per trip (cap Rs 50,000).
  5. Renewed/re-issued Indian passport after naturalisation and travelled: Rs 25,000 per renewal + Rs 10,000 per trip (cap Rs 50,000).
  Statutory sanction quoted: imprisonment 1–5 years and fine Rs 10,000–50,000 for a non-citizen obtaining a passport by suppressing nationality. Pretoria: "possession of Indian passport by foreign nationals is a cognizable offence" (https://hcipretoria.gov.in/docs/1722253913Revise.pdf). UNVERIFIED: the MHA/MEA order number and date behind the schedule, and whether US missions apply identical amounts in USD.
- **Downstream dependency:** Surrender Certificate (or renunciation certificate) is a mandatory document for OCI and for Indian visas; also "Affidavit of naturalization after passport expiry (gap letter)" if the Indian passport had expired before naturalisation (VFS checklist).
- **Classification:**
  - *Legally overdue:* T3 date on/after 01-Jun-2010 and Indian passport not yet surrendered; aggravated if any India trip on the Indian passport after T3 (penalty rows 2–5) or if the Indian passport was renewed after T3 (row 5).
  - *Action recommended:* T3 before 01-Jun-2010 with no cancellation stamp (will need proof for OCI); surrendered but certificate lost (consulate does not keep copies after Nov-2020 per CGI SF; re-apply).
  - *Information missing:* naturalisation date; Indian passport number, issue/expiry; whether it was renewed after naturalisation; India trips (dates, which passport used) after naturalisation; surrender certificate held?

## 7. OCI

### 7.1 Re-issue and passport-upload calendar

- **Trigger:** T3a plus each new foreign passport.
- **Rules (Embassy of India, Washington DC, "OCI Card Reissue Guidelines", https://indianembassyusa.gov.in/pages/NjI ; same text at https://www.indembassybern.gov.in/page/revised-guidelines-on-re-issue-of-oci-cards):**
  - Under 20: upload copy of each new passport + recent photo online every time a new passport is issued; gratis.
  - Re-issue required once, when a new passport is issued after completing 20 years of age ("OCI Miscellaneous Services"; fee $25 + $3 ICWF + $15.90 service; $100 if lost/damaged).
  - 21–50: no further action after the one post-20 re-issue.
  - After 50: upload new passport + photo once; gratis.
  - Foreign-origin spouse of Indian citizen / of OCI cardholder: upload new passport + photo every time, plus declaration that marriage subsists and copies of spouse's passport/OCI.
  - Other mandatory re-issue triggers: loss/damage, change of name (incl. marriage), change of nationality, errors.
  - Upload window: "within three months of receipt of new passport."
  - Travel: holder may travel with existing OCI card (old passport number) together with the new passport; no travel restriction stated.
- **Consequence of non-compliance:** none stated on the pages ("The page mentions no penalties for late uploads"). UNVERIFIED.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue:* a new passport was received >3 months ago and the applicable upload/re-issue (per age/spouse rule) was not done. *Action recommended:* re-issue pending for a 20+ holder whose card still shows a child photo; spouse-OCI with changed passport. *Information missing:* DOB; OCI issue date and passport number printed on it; current passport issue date; spouse-OCI flag.

### 7.2 OCI rights and restrictions (MHA notification 04-Mar-2021, consolidating 2005/2007/2009)

Source: https://hciottawa.gov.in/whatsnew?id=55 (links Gazette egazette.nic.in/WriteReadData/2021/225647.pdf); summary also at https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/general-information-on-oci-card/ (updated 27-Oct-2026).
- Lifelong multiple-entry multipurpose visa; exempt from FRRO registration for any length of stay.
- **Special permission required** (from competent authority/FRRO/Mission): research; "missionary, Tabligh, mountaineering, or journalist activities"; internship/employment with foreign diplomatic missions or foreign government organisations in India; visits to Protected/Restricted/Prohibited areas.
- **Parity with NRIs** in: inter-country adoption; all-India entrance exams (not seats reserved for citizens); "purchase or sale of immovable property" **except "agricultural land, farmhouses, or plantation properties"**; practice as doctor, dentist, nurse, pharmacist, lawyer, architect, CA.
- **Parity with Indian nationals:** domestic airfares; entry fees to monuments/parks/museums.
- All other economic/educational/financial matters: "at par with other foreign nationals".
- **Not permitted:** voting; legislature/Parliament membership; constitutional posts; "normally cannot hold government employment".
- **Cancellation grounds** (s.7D): fraud; disaffection to Constitution; aiding enemy in war; sentence ≥2 years within 5 years of registration; sovereignty/security/public interest. Renunciation of OCI by a parent terminates minor child's OCI.
- **Classification:** Restrictions are conduct rules, not filings: *action recommended* flag whenever intake shows agricultural land ownership intent, journalism/research/missionary work, or travel to NE/Ladakh/Andaman protected areas.

## 8. Property

### 8.1 Purchase: agricultural land / plantation / farmhouse prohibited for NRI and OCI

- **Trigger:** T1 (NRI) or T3a (OCI).
- **Rule:** RBI FAQ "Acquisition and transfer of immovable property in India" (updated 06-Apr-2023) https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=117 : NRI/OCI may "Purchase (other than agricultural land/ farmhouse/ plantation etc) from Resident/NRI/OCI" [NDI Rules r.24(a)]; may receive as gift "other than agricultural land/ farmhouse/ plantation" from a relative [24(b)]; may **sell or gift agricultural land only to a Resident** [24(d)]. MHA 2021 OCI notification repeats the exception (7.2). Foreign nationals of non-Indian origin resident outside India: only leases ≤5 years or inheritance from a resident; citizens of Pakistan, Bangladesh, China, Nepal, etc. need prior RBI permission (not applicable to OCI).
- **Consequence:** UNVERIFIED on official pages (FEMA s.13 contravention is the lead).
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue:* agricultural/plantation/farmhouse property **purchased or received as gift** after T1/T3a. *Action recommended:* inherited agricultural land held (permitted, see 9) — sale only to a resident; plan accordingly. *Information missing:* property type (agricultural vs residential/commercial), mode of acquisition (purchase/gift/inheritance), date vs T1 date.

### 8.2 TDS on sale of property by a non-resident (s.195)

- **Trigger:** T2 non-resident seller.
- **Rule (Department FAQ):** "the buyer is required to deduct tax at source (TDS) under Section 195". Long-term (held >24 months): 20% for transfers before 23-07-2024, 12.5% on/after; short-term: 30% (individual). Plus applicable surcharge and health & education cess. Rate is on the sale consideration unless a certificate specifies otherwise (FAQ silent on base — UNVERIFIED). https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/what-is-the-applicable-tds-rate-when-a-non-resident-sells-immovable-property-in-india-
- **Lower/nil deduction:** s.197(1) covers deduction "under the provisions of sections 192, 193, 194A, 194B, 194BB, 194D and 195"; the AO "shall, on an application made by the assessee in this behalf, give to him such certificate as may be appropriate" (Form 13; rule 28). https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-197-28 ; rule page https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-28-10 (not fetched).
- **Deadline:** TDS is deducted at credit/payment; certificate must be obtained *before* the sale deed/payment to be useful. UNVERIFIED: TAN requirement for buyer, Form 27Q timelines (not on fetched pages).
- **Consequence:** For the buyer: s.201 default. For the seller: over-withholding recoverable only via ITR refund (which itself requires a valid, operative PAN — see 3.2).
- **Classification:** *Action recommended* (pre-sale): obtain s.197 certificate; ensure PAN operative. *Legally overdue:* sale completed, TDS short-deducted and no ITR filed for the year of sale. *Information missing:* intended sale? holding period, acquisition cost/date, expected consideration.

### 8.3 Repatriation of sale proceeds / NRO balances

- **Rule:** Remittance of Assets Master Direction (FED MD 13/2015-16, updated 29-Jun-2026) para 3.2: NRI/PIO may remit "up to USD one million per financial year" out of "balances in their non-resident (ordinary) (NRO) accounts/ sale proceeds of assets/ assets acquired in India by inheritance or legacy" or under a settlement deed effective on the settlor's death, subject to applicable taxes and an undertaking. https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10197 . RBI property FAQ Q.7: for residential property, "repatriation of sale proceeds is restricted to not more than two such properties"; amounts originally paid from NRE/FCNR are repatriable outside the USD 1m cap per the FAQ's conditions. https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=117
- **Documentation:** UNVERIFIED on RBI pages (Form 15CA/15CB is the Income-tax lead; rule 37BB not fetched).
- **Classification:** Not an obligation; a *cap*. *Action recommended* if planned remittance in an Indian FY exceeds USD 1m (needs RBI approval) or if proceeds relate to a third+ residential property. *Information missing:* FY-wise remittance totals; source account; property count.

## 9. Inheritance

- **Receipt:** NRI/OCI may "Acquire (any IP) as inheritance from" any person who acquired it under laws in force, or from a resident [NDI r.24(c)] — **including agricultural land** (the inheritance row carries no agricultural exclusion). https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=117 . Foreign nationals of non-Indian origin may inherit from a resident.
- **Onward transfer:** inherited agricultural land may be sold/gifted only to a resident [r.24(d)].
- **Repatriation:** inherited assets fall under the USD 1m/FY facility (MD 13 para 3.2); a foreign national who is not PIO (e.g., a non-Indian-origin spouse) has a separate USD 1m/FY facility where assets were inherited from a person referred to in FEMA s.6(5) or from a deceased Indian-national spouse (para 3.1).
- **Indian tax:** no inheritance/estate tax statute located on official pages (none exists; UNVERIFIED by absence). Capital gains on later sale: s.195 TDS per 8.2.
- **US side:** bequest > $100,000 → Form 3520 Part IV (2.3); inherited accounts → FBAR/8938 (2.1/2.2).
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue:* inheritance in a closed US tax year > $100,000 without Form 3520; inherited Indian accounts omitted from FBAR/8938. *Action recommended:* inherited agricultural land (sale restricted to residents); succession paperwork (mutation, legal heir certificate — state law, UNVERIFIED). *Information missing:* parents/relatives in India with assets; deaths and dates; asset list and values at date of death.

## 10. Indian income-tax return, DTAA, TRC/Form 10F

### 10.1 ITR filing obligation

- **Trigger:** T2 non-resident with India-source income.
- **Rule:** "Return filing is mandatory if income taxable in India exceeds the basic exemption limit" (non-resident section, Department "Return of income" page, admin date 27-May-2026). The resident-only triggers (foreign assets/signing authority, s.139(1) proviso) do not extend to non-residents on that page. Basic exemption for AY 2026-27: Rs 4,00,000 under default new regime (s.115BAC); Rs 2,50,000 under old regime; non-residents get Rs 2,50,000 "irrespective of age" (no senior-citizen uplift) and the s.87A rebate text refers to "resident individual". Sources: https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/return-of-income ; https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tax-rates%E2%80%8B . Also relevant: filing is needed to claim refund of excess TDS (e.g., 8.2) or to claim DTAA rate.
- **Deadline:** 31 July following the FY ("Any other assessee"). Belated return up to 31 December of the AY (e.g., 31-Dec-2026 for AY 2026-27).
- **Consequence:** s.234F fee: Rs 5,000 (Rs 1,000 cap if total income ≤ Rs 5 lakh) — the page fetched shows the 2017 text including a Rs 10,000 tier; current operative amounts UNVERIFIED beyond that page https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-234f ; interest s.234A; loss of refund/DTAA claim if no return.
- **Classification:** *Legally overdue:* any FY after T2 in which India-source income (rent, interest on NRO, capital gains, dividends) exceeded the exemption limit and no ITR by 31 Dec of the AY. *Action recommended:* income below limit but TDS deducted (refund) or capital-gains year. *Information missing:* FY-wise Indian income by head; Form 26AS/AIS; regime choice.

### 10.2 India–US DTAA and TRC / Form 10F

- **Treaty:** Notification GSR 992(E) 20-12-1990 (corrected GSR 342(E) 12-07-1991); in force 18-Dec-1990. Art. 4 residence/tie-breaker; Art. 11 interest 10% (bank loans)/15% (other); Art. 13 gains taxable per domestic law of each state; Art. 20(1) private pensions taxable only in state of residence, 20(2) social security only in paying state; Art. 25 US credit for Indian tax. https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/usa-comprehensive-agreements-1
- **Claiming treaty rates in India:** a non-resident "shall be entitled to claim any relief under such DTAA only if he obtains a Tax Residency Certificate" from the foreign tax authority (s.90(4)), and must furnish Form 10F electronically (s.90(5), rule 21AB). Form 10F particulars: status, nationality, foreign TIN, period of residence certificate, foreign address; not required where already in the TRC (r.21AB(2)); keep supporting documents (r.21AB(2A)). https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/double-taxation-relief ; https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-21ab (uploaded 13-12-2025). US TRC = IRS Form 6166 (IRS source not fetched; UNVERIFIED here).
- **Classification:** Not an obligation; a *precondition* for lower withholding (e.g., NRO interest at 15% instead of 30%+). *Action recommended* if Indian payer is withholding at domestic rates on interest/dividends/pension. *Information missing:* income types; whether payer accepts TRC/10F; whether Form 6166 has been obtained for the year.
